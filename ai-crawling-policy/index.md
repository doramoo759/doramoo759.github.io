# AI & Crawling Policy

date: 2026-01-01

> Conditions for search indexing, automated collection, AI training, and dataset use.

This policy addresses automated access to ramoo.net content. Reuse and attribution requirements are set out in the [Content License](/license/).

## Search indexing

Ordinary search-engine crawling for indexing, search previews, and links to the original pages is permitted.

## Limited collection and AI use

Limited automated collection for technical review, partial quotations for personal study, and summaries that link to the original page are permitted under the Content License.

Use for AI training, dataset preparation, or text collection must identify the source and retain links to the original pages. Attribution alone does not authorize the bulk uses restricted below.

## Restricted uses

Without separate permission, do not:

- Collect the entire site or large numbers of pages in bulk, except for ordinary search indexing described above.
- Reproduce complete site content in a separate dataset, paid service, or closed knowledge base.
- Use content in AI answers, documents, or datasets without original source links.
- Send excessive requests that disrupt the site.
- Conceal the source or present the material as another author's work.

## Permission requests

For collection, training, or dataset uses that need separate permission, see the [Contact page](/contact/).
