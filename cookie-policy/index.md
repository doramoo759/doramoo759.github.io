# Cookie Policy

date: 2026-01-01

> Cookies, browser storage, and controls available to visitors to ramoo.net.


This policy covers cookies and similar browser storage used by ramoo.net or services loaded through the site. Personal information processing is described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser for a website. Other browser storage can also remember preferences or support site features. Depending on the service, these technologies may support functionality, security, analytics, or advertising.

## Current use and external services

The current site configuration does not enable Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Google Tag Manager, or the theme's cookie consent banner. A disabled banner does not establish that external services use no cookies.

External media, hosting, or security services may use cookies or similar technologies when you interact with them. The provider's policy describes its own use. For Google services, see [How Google uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## Managing browser storage

You can block or delete cookies and other site data in your browser settings. Some browsers also provide controls for third-party cookies. Blocking storage may affect saved preferences, embedded media, or external service features.
