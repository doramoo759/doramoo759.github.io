# Cookie Policy

date: 2026-01-01

> Analytics consent, cookies, browser storage, and preference controls on ramoo.net.

This policy explains cookies and browser storage on ramoo.net and how to manage analytics preferences. For information about personal information generally, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Analytics preferences

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept analytics. The site's Google analytics tag does not load before you make a choice or when you decline. You can change your choice below at any time. Declining does not prevent you from reading articles or using the site's main features.

{{< analytics-settings >}}

Declining stops subsequent analytics transmission and removes accessible Analytics cookies. It does not automatically delete records already sent to Google. An analytics script loaded before you decline may remain in the current tab's memory; it will not be loaded on subsequent pages. Other browser site data is not deleted.

## Current browser storage

| Item | Purpose | Retention and controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ramoo.analytics-consent.v1` in localStorage | Store your analytics choice and the time it was made | Valid for 180 days after your choice. You are asked again after expiration. Remove it by deleting browser site data. |
| `theme` in localStorage | Remember light or dark appearance | No automatic expiration. Change it with the theme control or remove it by deleting browser site data. |
| Analytics cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*` | Identify visits and handle sessions for visitors who accept analytics | Subject to Google's defaults and service settings. Google describes a two-year default lifetime for the main `_ga` cookie, which may be renewed. Decline analytics here or delete cookies in your browser. |

If browser storage is blocked, your analytics choice may apply only to the current page. If no explicit choice is saved and your browser sends Do Not Track, analytics is treated as declined.

Preferences are stored separately for each browser and site address. You may need to choose again on another device, browser, or hostname. Google AdSense and a separate Google Tag Manager container are not currently enabled. The analytics controls above replace the theme's default cookie banner.

References: [How Google uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies), [Google tag cookie configuration](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/customize-cookies).

## External services

- TradingView's official widget FAQ states that its widgets do not set cookies. Even without cookies, widget requests process information including your IP address and the embedding page. See the [official FAQ](https://www.tradingview.com/widget-docs/faq/general/#do-tradingview-widgets-set-cookies).
- Giscus and GitHub comment sign-in, Formspree inquiry handling, external videos, and security services may use storage subject to their providers' own policies. This does not mean that every provider uses the same cookies.
- The IP lookup tool does not store results in browser cookies or local storage. Map images and other resources may remain in the browser cache.

Declining analytics does not block all connections to external market widgets, maps, comments, or inquiry services. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for information about these providers.

## Browser controls

Use your browser's privacy or site-data settings to inspect or delete cookies and local storage. You can also use third-party cookie blocking controls. Deleting site data may reset saved theme and analytics preferences, and blocking storage may affect some external features.
