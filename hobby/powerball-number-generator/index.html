How the generator works

Choose five different white ball numbers from 1 to 69 and one red Powerball from 1 to 26. The red ball comes from a separate pool, so it can have the same number as a white ball. These are the rules described on the official Powerball website.

The generator uses your browser’s cryptographic random source. Each eligible white ball has an equal chance of selection within your chosen constraints; the red ball is selected separately. Fixed and excluded numbers only control which combinations the tool can generate. They do not improve the odds of winning. Generated picks stay in your browser and are not lottery entries.

Use the frequency window to explore the most recent 10, 30, or 100 drawings. White ball counts and red Powerball counts are calculated separately. Past frequency, long absences, and apparent patterns do not predict a future drawing.

Drawing schedule and result updates

Powerball drawings are scheduled for Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. Eastern Time, according to the official game information. Eastern Time follows U.S. daylight saving time; the next drawing shown above is a scheduled drawing, and delays are possible.

Results on this page come from the New York State Gaming Commission’s public Powerball dataset. Its metadata lists a posting frequency of three times weekly, without promising a precise posting time. This site checks at 11:25 p.m. on drawing nights, 12:25 a.m. the following day, and 9:25 a.m. daily, all in Eastern Time. These are collection targets; source publication, scheduled execution, and site deployment can be delayed. If collection fails, the last verified results and their original date remain visible.

The checker reports number matches against the latest result saved on this page. It does not validate tickets, Power Play eligibility, Double Play results, or prize claims. Always confirm results and prize eligibility with your lottery’s official records.

A note on independent drawings

For the current 5/69 + 1/26 format, the number of possible jackpot combinations is C(69, 5) × 26 = 292,201,338. Every valid combination has the same jackpot probability in a fair drawing.
