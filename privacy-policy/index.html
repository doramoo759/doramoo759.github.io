ramoo.net is a personal blog operated by ramoo. The site does not offer account registration. This policy explains information processed when you visit the site, submit an inquiry, use comments, or access external features.

Information and purposes

ActivityInformation processedPurpose
Page delivery and securityIP address, requested URL and time, browser, device, and connection informationDeliver pages and images, investigate errors, and prevent abuse
InquiriesName, email address, message, and reply recordsRespond to inquiries and follow-up questions

IP lookup toolPublic IP address, IP-based approximate location and network information, request headers, and connection informationDisplay information about your current connection

Do not include unnecessary sensitive information, passwords, or national identification numbers in inquiries or comments.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is configured for this site. The Google analytics tag loads only after you accept analytics. It does not load while you have not made a choice or have declined. Google Signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled in the site’s code.

You can change your choice in the Cookie Policy’s analytics preferences. Declining stops subsequent analytics transmission from this site and removes accessible Analytics cookies. It does not automatically delete information already transmitted. A browser’s Do Not Track signal is treated as a refusal when there is no explicitly saved choice.

Google AdSense and a separate Google Tag Manager container are not currently enabled. The analytics script’s googletagmanager.com address does not by itself mean that a Tag Manager container is in use. See the Google Privacy Policy and Analytics data protection information.

Inquiries and Formspree

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to Formspree, Inc. over HTTPS and used by the operator to respond. Submitting an inquiry is optional. Without consent, you cannot send an inquiry through the form.

Formspree states that it processes information in the United States and other countries where it operates. It may also process request information, such as IP address, browser, and access time, to prevent spam and abuse. See the Formspree Privacy Policy for its processing and international handling of information.

Inquiry records and reply emails managed by the site operator are deleted within 90 days after the inquiry has been answered. If you request deletion earlier, the request is handled after the minimum verification needed to confirm your identity. Copies in the Formspree inbox and the operator’s email are included. Provider-managed security or legally required logs and backups follow the provider’s own retention policies; deletion at the same time is not guaranteed.

Site delivery and external features

  • GitHub and Cloudflare: Connection information may be processed during site deployment, image delivery, API access, and security operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Provider-managed log retention depends on the service and its actual settings.
  • TradingView: Opening the homepage connects to TradingView to load market widgets. Its official FAQ states that widgets do not set cookies and process the embedding page URL, widget type, displayed symbol, and IP address to provide the widget. See the widget FAQ and Privacy Policy. Declining analytics does not block this market-data connection.
  • IP lookup API: Opening the IP lookup page or refreshing the lookup sends a request to https://api.ramoo.net/ip to display your public IP and approximate location, network, and connection information. Results are not saved in browser cookies or local storage, and the lookup request omits browser credentials. This does not mean that the API or hosting layer keeps no access logs. Such logs are subject to the API and hosting providers’ operating and retention policies.
  • OpenStreetMap: When an IP-based approximate location is available, the map fetches tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org. Requests may disclose your IP address, request headers, and the displayed area indicated by tile coordinates to the map provider. The feature does not request browser GPS permission. See the OSMF Privacy Policy.
  • Giscus and GitHub comments: Loading the comment widget connects to Giscus. Posting requires GitHub sign-in and authorization. Comments and reactions are stored in public GitHub Discussions, where other visitors can see your public profile and posts. See Giscus and the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Other external content: Loading external videos or resources in articles, or following external links, may send connection information to those providers. Their own policies apply.

Retention, deletion, and your requests

The operator deletes inquiries from the Formspree inbox and email under the 90-day rule above. Your analytics choice is saved in your browser with a validity period of 180 days. Theme settings and cookies are described in the Cookie Policy. Retention of analytics on Google’s servers and logs held by external providers depends on account settings and provider policies.

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent, or to ask a privacy question, use Contact and describe your request. The operator will verify only the minimum information needed to confirm your identity, handle the request, and respond. You can withdraw analytics consent directly on the Cookie Policy page. You can edit or delete public GitHub comments through your GitHub account’s comment controls or contact the operator.

Privacy contact: ramoo, through the Contact page. Do not send someone else’s personal information or unnecessary copies of identification documents.
