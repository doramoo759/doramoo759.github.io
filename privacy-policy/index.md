# Privacy Policy

date: 2026-01-01

> How ramoo.net handles visitor information and third-party services.


This policy explains how ramoo.net handles information related to visits and inquiries. The site does not offer account registration.

## Information processed

Hosting, CDN, and security providers may process IP addresses, requested URLs, access times, browser and device information, and connection logs when delivering and protecting the site.

If you voluntarily send an inquiry, the information you provide is used to respond to it.

The contact form sends your name, email address, and message to Formspree to deliver the inquiry to the site operator. Formspree's handling of this information is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Purposes

Information may be processed to deliver pages, maintain the site, investigate errors, prevent abuse, and respond to inquiries. Visitor statistics or advertising may involve additional processing if those services are enabled.

Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and Google Tag Manager are not enabled in the current site configuration. This policy will be updated if the site's use of these services changes.

## Third-party services

Information handled by external providers is also subject to their own policies. Depending on the hosting and delivery setup, relevant policies may include the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Loading externally hosted resources, embedded media, or linked services may send connection information to those providers. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

## Comments

Where Giscus comments are enabled, comments and reactions are stored in public GitHub Discussions. Posting requires GitHub sign-in and authorization through Giscus. Your public GitHub profile and the content you post are visible to other visitors. Loading the comment widget connects your browser to Giscus; signing in also involves GitHub. These services are subject to their own policies, including the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Do not include private information in a public comment.

## Storage and retention

Retention of provider-managed access logs depends on the provider's settings and policies. No single retention period is specified here for all external services.

Browser storage and the controls available to visitors are described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## Questions and changes

For privacy questions, see the [Contact page](/contact/). Updates to this policy will be published here.
